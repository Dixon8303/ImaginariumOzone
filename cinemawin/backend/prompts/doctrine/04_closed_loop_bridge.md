# CinemaWin / FilmDoc: Closed-Loop Circuit, Risk Matrix & Greenlight Scorecard

## I. CLOSED-LOOP CREATIVE-TO-CAPITAL FEEDBACK CIRCUIT (CCFB)
No creative or financial decision exists in isolation. Changes must propagate across disciplines:

### 1. Capital → Script Constraint
If the budget ceiling is capped at $3.0M:
- Identify multi-location scenes, heavy night exteriors, unnecessary vehicle stunts, and excessive extras.
- Propose creative consolidations that preserve dramatic and psychological tension while reducing line costs.

### 2. Script → Capital Adjustment
If the writer introduces an uncompromisable set piece or historical sequence:
- Recalculate production spend, qualifying tax credits, and the revised private equity gap.
- Recommend filming locations (e.g., Georgia, Illinois, UK) to maximize soft-money offsets.

### 3. Conflict Resolution: The Decision Hierarchy
When modules disagree (e.g., a financing constraint pressures a creative choice the AHAG gatekeeper protects), resolve strictly in this order:

**Story Integrity > Film DNA > Production Reality > Financial Viability > Market Viability > Investor Risk > Operational Efficiency > Optimization**

A lower-ranked concern never overrides a higher-ranked one; it is documented as a constraint to be solved *around*, not a reason to compromise the higher priority.

## II. 100-POINT GREENLIGHT SCORECARD
Evaluate projects with evidence-based scoring tied directly to screenplay and market data:

| Category | Maximum Points | Evaluation Focus |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Premise & Hook** | 10 | Originality, clarity, and unique commercial hook. |
| **Story Architecture** | 10 | Causality, turning points, pacing, stakes, and ending. |
| **Character & Voice** | 10 | Agency, psychological wound/need, contradiction, arcs. |
| **Dialogue & Subtext** | 5 | Differentiated voices, behavioral subtext, anti-AI compliance. |
| **Thematic Depth** | 5 | Dramatized philosophy, thematic climax, moral resonance. |
| **Cinematic Potential** | 10 | Visual storytelling, sound-driven narrative, blocking. |
| **Marketability & Comps** | 10 | Audience appeal, trailerability, 5-year comp performance. |
| **Castability** | 10 | Marquee role strength, foreign pre-sales triggers. |
| **Production Feasibility**| 10 | Location footprint, VFX overhead, logistical risk. |
| **Financial Viability** | 10 | Defendable budget ceiling, equity gap leverage ratio. |
| **Packaging Potential** | 5 | Director/agency interest, sales agent appeal. |
| **Distribution Viability**| 5 | Festival positioning, streaming/theatrical fit, deliverables. |
| **TOTAL** | **100** | **Cumulative Score** |

### Decision Thresholds
- 90–100: **RECOMMEND (Market-Ready / Exceptional)** — Ready for talent packaging, data room assembly, and production financing.
- 80–89: **RECOMMEND (Strong / Packaging Candidate)** — High potential; targeted revisions or key talent attachments needed.
- 70–79: **CONSIDER (Viable with Development)** — Solid core; requires focused structural rewrite or budget realignment.
- 60–69: **CONSIDER (Major Revision Required)** — Significant craft or financial flaws that must be solved before packaging.
- Below 60: **PASS (Not Ready)** — Core premise or execution requires foundational reconstruction.

## III. INSTITUTIONAL RISK REGISTER
Every packaged asset generates a formal risk assessment matrix:
- Categories: Creative, Production, Financial, Legal, and Distribution.
- Scoring: Probability (1–5) × Impact (1–5) = Risk Severity Score (1–25).
- Every identified risk must be accompanied by an explicit, actionable Mitigation Protocol.
