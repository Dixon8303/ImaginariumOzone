# CinemaWin / FilmDoc: Film Finance, Packaging & Legal Clearances

## I. DATA-DRIVEN BUDGET CEILINGS & COMPS
- Benchmark against 3–5 recent market comparables within the last 5 years of equivalent genre, cast size, and physical scale.
- Establish defendable budget ceilings based on acquisition trends:
  - Micro / Ultra-Low: <$500K
  - Contained Indie Tier: $1.5M – $3.0M
  - Mid-Tier Indie / Streamer Buyout: $4.0M – $7.0M
  - Studio Independent / Prestige: $10.0M – $15.0M+

---

## II. THE 4-LAYER CAPITAL STACK
Every capital structure must be mathematically balanced:

$$\text{Total Production Budget} = \text{Tax Incentives} + \text{Pre-Sales/MGs} + \text{Subsidies/Grants} + \text{Calculated Equity Gap}$$

1. Layer 1: Tax Incentives / Soft Money (20%–35% of qualifying in-state spend): Sourced from jurisdictions such as Georgia (30%), Illinois (30%), California, New Jersey, or the UK. Monetized via bridge loans at $0.88–$0.92 on the dollar.
2. Layer 2: Foreign Pre-Sales & Minimum Guarantees (15%–30%): Contractual MGs brokered across major foreign territories (UK, Germany/Benelux, France, LATAM, Japan) contingent on Tier-1 talent LOIs.
3. Layer 3: Brand Integration & Grants (5%–10%): Corporate product placement, hardware/camera sponsorships, regional arts and cultural foundation grants.
4. Layer 4: Calculated Equity Gap (35%–50%): The net private equity request. Equity investors enter with 50%+ of budget risk de-risked by non-dilutive capital prior to camera roll.

---

## III. INVESTOR WATERFALL RECOUPMENT MODEL
Revenues collected by the production entity flow strictly in institutional priority order:
1. Gross Receipts
2. International Sales Agent Commission (10%–25%) + approved delivery expenses
3. Senior Debt & Tax Credit Bridge Loan payoff (+ accrued interest)
4. Equity Investor Capital Recoupment + 110%–120% Preferred Return (pari-passu)
5. Net Profits: 50% to Equity Investor Pool / 50% to Producer & Creative Talent Pool

## IV. CAST PACKAGING MATRIX
- Tier 1 (Global Financing Triggers): Recognizable names whose attachment yields bankable foreign minimum guarantees.
- Tier 2 (Domestic / SVOD Triggers): High domestic streaming recognition driving platform bidding wars.
- Tier 3 (Prestige / Festival Darlings): Critical acclaim; increases A-list festival entry probability (Sundance, TIFF, SXSW).

Mechanics: Secure talent via binding Pay-or-Play Letters of Intent (LOIs) with escrow contingencies.

## VI. RIGHTS & AUTHORITY AUDIT (RAA) & CLEARANCES
- Underlying Rights: Verify life rights, chain of title, option agreements, copyright registrations, and certificate of authorship.
- Action Line Clearance Engine: Scan script text for real trademarks, corporate brands, defamatory references, songs, artwork, and recognizable public figures. Classify as LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, or CRITICAL risk; assign remedies (Clearance, Fictionalization, Greeking, or Removal).

## VII. DEPARTMENTAL TOP-SHEET BUDGET ENGINE (DTBE)
*(v2.0 addition — drafted using standard industry top-sheet structure to give PPBE's physical breakdown a dollar figure; not sourced from original spec text, treat as DRAFT pending your review.)*

Converts the PPBE breakdown (Module 02, Section I) into a department-by-department top sheet. Every PPBE element tag (CAST, STUNTS, EXTRAS, PRACTICAL SFX, VFX, VEHICLES, PROPS, SOUND) must map to at least one top-sheet line; an unmapped PPBE element is a budget gap, not an omission to ignore.

**Above-the-Line:** Story/Rights, Producers, Director, Cast (principal deals, not day-player extras).

**Below-the-Line — Production:** Production Staff, Extra Talent/Background, Set Design, Set Construction, Set Operations (grip/electric), Special Effects (practical), Set Dressing, Property, Wardrobe, Makeup & Hair, Camera, Production Sound, Transportation, Locations, Picture Vehicles & Animals, Production Film/Digital Media & Lab.

**Below-the-Line — Post-Production:** Editorial, Music, Post Sound (mix, ADR, Foley), Titles/Opticals/VFX, Post Film/Digital Media & Lab, Delivery (feeds Module 03's Distribution Delivery Manifest, formerly DDM).

**Other:** Insurance & Completion Bond, General & Administrative, Publicity, Contingency.

**Rules:**
- Contingency defaults to 10% of the above-the-line + below-the-line subtotal unless a `LOW-BUDGET` or `INVESTOR-FIRST` lens specifies otherwise.
- Labor line items carry a fringe rate (payroll tax, union benefits where applicable) as a visible sub-line, never buried in the base rate.
- Every top-sheet total must reconcile against the budget ceiling established in Section I; a top sheet that exceeds the ceiling triggers CCFB (Module 04, Section I) rather than silent inflation of the ceiling.
