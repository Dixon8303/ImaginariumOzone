# CinemaWin / FilmDoc: Physical Production Breakdown & Lookbook Architect

## I. PHYSICAL PRODUCTION BREAKDOWN ENGINE (PPBE)
Convert the screenplay into an operational physical production breakdown:

### 1. Scene Parsing Rules
- Scene Heading: INT / EXT, Location, Time of Day (DAY / NIGHT / MAGIC HOUR).
- Measurement: Length measured strictly in eighths of a page (1/8 to 8/8).
- Story Day: Chronological timeline tracking within the narrative world.

### 2. Element Tagging Standard
- CAST: Assigned unique Cast IDs (C01, C02, etc.). Track Lead, Supporting, Day Players, Voice, Background.
- STUNTS: Combat, falls, precision driving, wirework, weapons handling, hazardous environments.
- EXTRAS / ATMOSPHERE: Exact headcount, crowd duplication needs, specialized background wardrobe.
- PRACTICAL SFX: Squibs, pyrotechnics, atmospheric smoke, practical blood, structural breakaway sets.
- VFX CUES: Set extensions, digital crowd duplication, monitor burn-ins, cleanups, dynamic CGI elements.
- VEHICLES: Picture cars, stunt doubles, period vehicles, specialized process trailers.
- PROPS: Hero props, actionable hand props, food/perishables, period hand props.
- SOUND CHALLENGES: Playback, multi-mic setups, acoustic anomalies, heavy background noise environments.

## II. CAST DAY-OUT-OF-DAYS (DOOD) ENGINE
Track every Cast ID across scheduled production days:
- Status Codes: W (Work), T (Travel), H (Hold), S (Start), F (Finish).
- Waste Auditing: Flag idle hold days between work dates. If Cast C03 works Day 2 and Day 8, audit the financial impact of 5 hold days versus scheduling scenes consecutively.

---

## III. LOCATION FOOTPRINT & CONSOLIDATION
Map every physical set to a primary production hub:
- Cluster rule: Confine 75%–85% of shooting days to 1–3 primary interior/exterior hubs.
- Audit company moves: Every physical location change per day burns 2 to 3.5 hours of crew turnaround. Minimize moves to protect shooting hours.

## IV. DIRECTOR'S LOOKBOOK ARCHITECT (LVA)
1. Aspect Ratio: 2.39:1 (Scope/Anamorphic tension/distance), 1.85:1 (Grounded human intimacy), 1.66:1 / 4:3 (Psychological confinement).
2. Lens Philosophy: Spherical sharpness vs. Anamorphic falloff; wide-angle environmental distortion vs. long-lens spatial compression.
3. Camera Movement: Handheld (psychological instability), Steadicam/Gimbal (fluid pursuit), Dolly (mechanized inevitability), Locked-Off (claustrophobia/observation).
4. Lighting Strategy: Key-to-fill ratios, practical/motivated sources, skin-tone color temperature arcs across Act I, II, and III.
5. Sonic Palette: Ambient environmental frequencies, room tone textures, silence as punctuation, and character leitmotifs.
