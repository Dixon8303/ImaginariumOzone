Privacy Policy

Applies to: Black Genius Files Content Engine · Effective 13 August 2026

Summary. The Black Genius Files Content Engine is a private, single-user automation script operated by its owner to publish Pins to that same owner's Pinterest boards. It has no other users. It collects no personal information from anyone. It is not a public product, a hosted service, or a reseller of any third-party API.

1. Who operates this tool

The Black Genius Files Content Engine ("the Tool") is built and operated by D. Antione Dixon, trading as E.A.T. Media ("we", "us"), publisher of the Black Genius Files. The Tool is used exclusively to publish promotional Pins for self-published books authored and owned by the operator.

2. Who uses this tool

One person: the operator. The Tool is not distributed, licensed, sold, or made available to any other individual or organization. There is no sign-up, no user account system, no customer base, and no end users other than the operator. It authenticates against a single Pinterest account — the operator's own.

3. What data the tool accesses

Through the Pinterest API, and only for the operator's own Pinterest account, the Tool accesses:

  • Boards (boards:read, boards:write) — the names and IDs of the operator's own boards, in order to select the correct destination board.
  • Pins (pins:read, pins:write) — creation of new Pins on the operator's own boards, and read-back of those Pins to confirm publication and prevent duplicate posts.

The Tool does not access, request, scrape, or store data belonging to any other Pinterest user. It does not read other users' Pins, boards, followers, profiles, or engagement data.

4. What data is stored, and where

  • An OAuth 2.0 access token and refresh token for the operator's own Pinterest account, stored in an encrypted local credential store on the operator's own machine. Tokens are never transmitted to any third party.
  • A local log of Pin IDs and post timestamps, used solely for duplicate prevention.
  • The image files and captions for the operator's own book promotions.

No data is stored on any public server, shared host, or cloud database. No analytics, tracking pixels, advertising identifiers, or third-party SDKs are used.

5. What data is shared

None. No data obtained from the Pinterest API is sold, rented, licensed, transferred, syndicated, or disclosed to any third party for any purpose. The Tool has no data-sharing partners, no advertisers, and no downstream integrations.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are retained only while the Tool is in active use and are revoked immediately if the Tool is retired. The local Pin log is retained for the life of the project and may be deleted at any time by the operator with no external effect. The operator may revoke the Tool's access at any time from Pinterest account settings, which permanently terminates all data access.

7. Children

The Tool is not directed to children, has no users other than its operator, and collects no information from anyone under 13.

8. Compliance

The Tool is operated in accordance with the Pinterest Developer Guidelines, the Pinterest Terms of Service, and the Pinterest API Terms. It does not resell, proxy, sublicense, or otherwise provide Pinterest API access to third parties. All content published is original work owned by the operator.

9. Changes to this policy

Any material change will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
