## 1. The Four Platform Pillars

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        EMERGENCE ENGINE PILLARS                       │
├───────────────────┬───────────────────┬────────────────┬───────────────┤
│ 1. Deep Substrate │ 2. Autonomous     │ 3. Massive     │ 4. Catalyst   │
│    Physics        │    Cognition      │    Scalability │    Intervention│
└───────────────────┴───────────────────┴────────────────┴───────────────┘
```

### Pillar 1: Deep Substrate Physics
The world is constructed of fundamental matter, energy, and entropy dynamics. Ecosystems and biomes form because temperature, moisture, and chemical energy flow strictly according to conservation laws.

### Pillar 2: Autonomous Cognition & Meme-Vectors
Agents possess utility-driven decision-making models. They do not follow fixed decision trees. Knowledge, culture, and beliefs are represented as quantitative **Meme-Vectors** that mutate and transmit through social interaction.

### Pillar 3: Massive Scalability
The architecture prioritizes data layout and CPU cache alignment. Simulation algorithms are vectorizable and scale linearly across multi-core systems.

### Pillar 4: Catalyst Intervention Mechanics
The player (or host application) does not act as a direct unit commander. The player acts as a **Catalyst**—manipulating environment variables, injecting energy, or introducing ideas into agent cognitive streams, observing how systems adapt.
