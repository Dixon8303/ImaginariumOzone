# Research Notes: Emergent Dynamics in Deterministic Substrates

---

## Abstract
Notes on modeling complex adaptive systems using pure data-oriented deterministic cellular structures and agent utility loops, and on what makes such systems readable and compelling to a player (DEOS-Play).

Key reference papers:
1. Wolfram, S. *A New Kind of Science* (Cellular Automata Complexity).
2. Langton, C. *Computation at the Edge of Chaos*.
3. Miller, J. & Page, S. *Complex Adaptive Systems*.

References relevant to DEOS-Play:
4. Csikszentmihalyi, M. (1990). *Flow: The Psychology of Optimal Experience*. New York: Harper & Row. Read for the balance of challenge and skill that the Catalyst Budget and `SPEED_LEVELS` pace, and for the requirement that feedback be immediate and legible (Directive 11, GI-5).
5. Schell, J. (2008). *The Art of Game Design: A Book of Lenses*. Burlington, MA: Morgan Kaufmann. Read for the lenses of curiosity, surprise, and endogenous value that the Chronicle, Seed Lineage, and World Phases serve, and for the lens of the "toy" that an unscripted substrate must first satisfy before it is a game.
