Step 02 — The Mechanism

Erasure is not weather. It is engineering.

A name does not slip from the record. It is removed — by someone, at some moment, for some reason. Four ordinary jobs do nearly all of it, and not one of them requires a conspiracy.

MECHANISM 01

The Clerk

Files one ship's log and throws away another. The Union's own reports on the Combahee raid were never found.

MECHANISM 02

The Editor

Puts one name in the headline and folds the rest into and others. A legend grows in the gap and undercounts the work.

MECHANISM 03

The Committee

Picks the textbook a generation will open — and what that generation will therefore never think to ask about.

MECHANISM 04

The Publisher

Decides which pages that textbook can afford to keep. Cost, not malice, does the final cutting.

None of them believe they are deciding the shape of history. Each of them is.
