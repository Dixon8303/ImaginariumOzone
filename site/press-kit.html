SYNOPSIS

Twelve stories the world’s most powerful institutions tried to erase — reopened, documented, and read back into the record. From the emperor whose gold moved world markets before Europe’s maps blanked him out, to the mathematician a machine answered to, to the singer a government spent seven years trying to silence.

The book’s claim is simple and severe: history does not go missing by accident. Erasure is engineering — and it proves it the only way such a claim can honestly be proven: with the record itself.

INSIDE THE VOLUME

  • Twelve case files, each anchored to primary documents — dispatches, patents, transcripts, court rulings
  • Evidence plates drawn from the archival record
  • “Struck & Corrected” — where the legend outran the evidence, the number is struck and the record printed
  • A primary-source appendix for every case

ABOUT THE AUTHOR

D. Antione Dixon is a Los Angeles media producer, filmmaker, educator, and author. Executive Producer of E.A.T. Media and Boombox Pictures, he directed the festival-selected documentary GodFood and hosts the All Black Everything Podcast, heard in more than 22 countries. What History Buried is his second book.
