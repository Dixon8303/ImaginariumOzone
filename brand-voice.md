## VOICE RULES
- Persona: **a professor with edge who teaches through story.** Authoritative but never stuffy; every big idea arrives wrapped in narrative — hook, tension, payoff.
- Funny ↔ Serious: serious about the ideas; wit and edge welcome, jokes never at the reader's expense
- Bold ↔ Humble: bold about the ideas and the research, humble about myself
- Casual ↔ Polished: polished but human — clear beats clever, and it never sounds like a lecture hall
- Short ↔ Storyteller: short punchy hooks up front, but story is the engine — one full narrative post per week minimum
- NEVER: talk down to the reader (they deserve respect and thoughtfulness) · hype or fake urgency · academic jargon or credential-flexing · politics and culture war (even when a topic brushes against it — teach the history, skip the fight) · begging, desperation, or apologizing for selling my own work

## INSTAGRAM
- Hook = first line, max 8 words (assume it's cut off after that)
- Short lines, white space, 0-2 emoji max
- MAX 5 hashtags, niche ones (#blackhistory #multipleintelligences #bookstagram-adjacent), at the very end
- Formats: Reels (30s, one buried-history story or one genius-profile insight per reel), carousels (slide 1 = one bold claim; slides 2-9 = the story or framework; last slide = CTA), quote graphics from the books
- Visual style: clean, bookish, dignified — real pages, real research, no stocky perfection

## X / TWITTER
- Every post ≤280 characters. One idea, sharpened. Bold claims backed by the books' research.
- No hashtags (or one, max)
- Threads: first tweet must work alone as a hook ("History buried this man twice."); number the rest; tell it like a story — tension before payoff; end with ONE CTA
- Edge is welcome here; disrespect is not. Challenge ideas, never people.

## LINKEDIN
- 150-300 words, one-line paragraphs, white space
- Angle: *The Genius Index* is the lead — careers, leadership, education, and why one-number intelligence fails organizations
- Professor-with-edge fits this room perfectly: authoritative, story-driven, zero jargon
- Max 3 hashtags. No links in the post body — "link in comments"

## TIKTOK / YOUTUBE SHORTS
- 30-second scripts: spoken words + on-screen text
- Hook in the FIRST 2 seconds, visual and specific ("School never taught you this name." / "Your IQ score is measuring the wrong thing.")
- Buried-history stories are the natural franchise here — one story per video, cliffhanger to the book
- Casual delivery, serious substance; slightly less slang on Shorts than TikTok
- End card: book cover + "link in bio"
