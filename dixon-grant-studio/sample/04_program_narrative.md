# Program Narrative — Rise & Read Watts

*(Mapped matrix rows: N1, N2, N3, N4, N5, N6, N9, N11, E3)*

## Who is served, and how (N6, E3)

Rise & Read Watts serves 120 K–5 students across Site A and Site B (60
per site), selected each fall through classroom-teacher referral cross-
checked against each site's benchmark reading assessment, prioritizing
students reading one or more full grade levels below standard. Both
sites are Title I schoolwide programs with free-or-reduced meal
eligibility above 85%, and enrollment is open to all qualifying students
regardless of English-learner, IEP, or foster/homeless status — no
student is turned away for a need the program is equipped to
accommodate with a classroom or district-provided aide.

## Program design and schedule (N1, N2, N3)

The program runs four days a week, 90 minutes per day (3:00–4:30 p.m.,
immediately following dismissal), for 34 weeks during the school year,
plus a 4-week, 3-hour/day summer bridge session to prevent
summer-reading-loss regression. Each 90-minute session is structured in
three blocks:

1. **Small-group phonics instruction (35 min)** — groups of 5–6 students
   matched by skill level, not grade level, using a structured,
   sequenced phonics curriculum aligned to each site's core literacy
   benchmark assessment.
2. **Guided independent reading (35 min)** — students read
   level-matched texts with a tutor providing real-time correction and
   comprehension check-ins, building both fluency and stamina.
3. **Choice reading and reflection (20 min)** — students choose their own
   books from a leveled classroom library and complete a brief written or
   verbal reflection, giving students voice in what they read and
   building sustained independent-reading habits.

Every session takes place in a safe, consistent, adult-supervised space
on each school campus — the same classroom or library each day, staffed
at a 1-tutor-to-5/6-student ratio, well below the district's licensed
minimum ratio for supervised youth programming.

## Student voice (N4)

Students select their own choice-reading titles weekly and vote quarterly
on the family-literacy-night theme (favorite-book character day, author
visit, bilingual storytelling night), giving students real input into
programming rather than a purely adult-designed schedule.

## Health and wellbeing (N5)

Each session opens with a 5-minute movement break (stretching or a
structured game) before instruction begins, and the program provides a
light, nutritious snack consistent with district wellness-policy
nutrition standards — addressing the reality that a hungry, unmoving
student after a full school day cannot sustain 90 minutes of focused
literacy work.

## Partnerships (N9)

The program operates under a signed site-use agreement with each school
(see `08_organizational_capacity.md` and attachment A5), with the site
principal or assistant principal as the day-to-day school-side point of
contact, joint quarterly data-review meetings between Rise & Read Watts
staff and each site's literacy coach, and six annual family literacy
nights held on each campus in partnership with each site's parent-teacher
organization.

## Day-to-day operations (N11)

The Program Director oversees both sites, the Literacy Coordinator
manages curriculum fidelity and tutor coaching, and each site maintains a
lead tutor responsible for daily attendance, safety check-in/check-out
procedures matching each school's existing after-school safety protocol,
and weekly data entry into the program's tracking system (see
`07_evaluation_plan.md`).
