## Organization

- **Name:** Rise & Read Watts
- **Structure:** 501(c)(3), incorporated 2019
- **Service area:** Watts / Willowbrook corridor, South Los Angeles
  (LAUSD Local District South)
- **Annual operating budget (current year):** $350,000
- **Mission:** Close the early-grade reading gap for children in Watts
  through structured, small-group literacy instruction outside the school
  day, delivered by trained tutors alongside classroom teaching.

## Program

- **Model:** After-school literacy intervention, 4 days/week, 90 minutes/
  day, 34-week school-year program plus a 4-week summer bridge session.
- **Sites:** Two Title I elementary school sites in the Watts/Willowbrook
  corridor, referred to here as **Site A** and **Site B** (names withheld
  — this is a fictional sample; no real school partnership exists or is
  implied).
- **Students served:** 120 K–5 students (60 per site), selected by
  classroom teacher referral and site reading-benchmark data, prioritizing
  students reading one or more grade levels below benchmark.
- **Curriculum:** Structured phonics-based small-group instruction
  (groups of 5–6 students per tutor) plus guided independent reading,
  aligned to each site's core literacy benchmark assessment.
- **Staffing:** 2.0 FTE (1 Program Director, 1 Literacy Coordinator) plus
  10 part-time trained tutors (paid, background-checked, minimum 8 hours/
  week each) and a rotating pool of 6 community reading-mentor volunteers
  for family literacy nights.

## Program data (most recent complete program year)

- 118 of 120 enrolled students completed ≥80% of scheduled sessions
- Entering reading level: average 1.8 grade levels below benchmark
- Exiting reading level: 71% of students gained ≥1 full grade level in
  benchmark reading score; 34% gained ≥1.5 grade levels
- Family literacy nights: 6 held, average attendance 84 family members
- Parent satisfaction survey: 92% "satisfied" or "very satisfied" (n=104
  respondents)
