## Budget narrative

**Classified Salaries ($199,600).** The Program Director (1.0 FTE)
oversees both sites, holds the funder and school-site relationships, and
carries the fiscal and compliance reporting described in
`08_organizational_capacity.md`. The Literacy Coordinator (1.0 FTE)
manages curriculum fidelity, leads tutor coaching, and administers the
three annual benchmark assessment windows described in
`07_evaluation_plan.md`. Ten part-time tutors deliver the small-group
phonics and guided-reading blocks described in `04_program_narrative.md`
at the funded 1:5–6 ratio, averaging 10 hours/week across the 38-week
program calendar (34 school-year weeks plus 4 summer-bridge weeks).

**Employee Benefits ($27,275).** Statutory benefits (health, retirement
contribution, workers' compensation) for the two full-time staff, and
employer payroll tax obligations for part-time tutor wages.

**Books and Supplies ($18,000).** Curriculum materials fund the
structured phonics program referenced in Program Narrative section N3;
leveled classroom libraries fund the choice-reading block (N4); program
snacks fund the wellness component (N5). No item in this category funds
an activity not described in the Program Narrative.

**Services & Other Operating Expenses ($12,000).** Background checks are
a program-management requirement (N11) for all tutor-facing staff before
they work with students. The data-tracking subscription funds the
evaluation plan's three-times-yearly assessment data collection. Family
literacy night costs fund the partnership activities described under N9.
Tutor professional development funds ongoing fidelity to the phonics
curriculum.

**Capital Outlay ($0).** No equipment or capital purchases requested this
cycle; existing classroom space and furnishings at both sites are used
under the signed site-use agreements (see attachment A5).

**Indirect Costs ($25,688).** Applied at the 10% de minimis rate to
modified total direct costs, consistent with Rise & Read Watts' current
federally negotiated (or de minimis-elected) indirect cost treatment —
`[MODELED — confirm against CDE's Cohort 16 guidance on allowable
indirect cost rates at RFA release]`.

**No duplicate funding (E4).** Rise & Read Watts' current active grants
fund general operating and a separate summer-only program at a third,
non-Title-I site; no other current award funds the specific staffing,
curriculum, or site activities described in this budget for Sites A and
B.
