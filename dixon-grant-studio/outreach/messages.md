# Five First-Contact Outreach Messages

Each message: honest, asks for one real trial job at $750, ends with a
single yes/no question. Bracketed placeholders stand in for real names
until the founder fills them in.

---

## 1. Watts of Power Foundation leadership

**To:** [Watts of Power Foundation Program Officer Name]
**Subject:** A grant-writing offer, from one of your Teaching Fellows

Hi [Name],

Being a Watts of Power Teaching Fellow put me inside a lot of small
education organizations doing real work without budget for a full-time
grant writer. I've built an MBA-trained, festival-tested process for
turning an RFP into a submission-ready application, scored against the
funder's own rubric before it ships — and I want to run it on one real
application, at a reduced $750 trial rate.

I'm not asking the Foundation to pay or vouch for this sight unseen —
just whether you know one CBO in your network with a live grant deadline
who'd want a second set of hands on it.

Is there someone you'd introduce me to?

## 3. Partner nonprofit serving the founder's school community

**To:** [Partner Organization Contact Name], [Organization Name]
**Subject:** Grant-writing help for [Organization Name]

Hi [Name],

I work inside a Title I elementary school in Watts as an instructional
aide, which is how I know the work [Organization Name] does for
families in this community. Separately, I've spent years building
investor pitch materials for my own documentary films and hold an MBA in
marketing — and I've turned that into a grant-writing process: a full
application package, scored against the funder's own rubric before
delivery.

I'd like to offer [Organization Name] a $750 trial run on one real,
upcoming application — full package, no obligation beyond that one job.
To be clear, this is entirely separate from my school role; I'm not
representing the school or district here.

Is there a grant deadline on your calendar this could help with?
