Booking & Payment Terms

  • Consultation

    Free 20-minute discovery call. A $75 hold reserves a 60-minute strategy session, credited to your booking.

  • Retainer

    A 50% non-refundable retainer confirms your date. The date is not held until the retainer clears.

  • Balance

    Due before your gallery or master files are released. Individuals pay in full at delivery; organizations are Net 15.

  • Late payment

    1.5% per month after Net 15. Delivery is paused on any past-due balance.

  • Reschedule

    Free once, with 72+ hours' notice. Inside 72 hours, the retainer is forfeited.

  • Cancellation

    The retainer is non-refundable in all cases. Within 7 days of a booked day, 100% of the fee is due.

  • Turnaround

    Photo proofs in 3–5 business days; retouched selects in 7–10. Video in 10–14 business days from receipt of all assets.

  • Revisions

    Photo: 1 round of retouch notes per image. Video: 2 rounds included, then $125/round.

  • Overtime

    Photography beyond your booked window bills at the hourly event rate, in 30-minute increments. Video beyond the day window bills at the hourly rate.

  • Usage rights

    E.A.T. Media retains copyright. You receive personal + portfolio use. Commercial use requires the licensing add-on above.

  • Portfolio rights

    E.A.T. Media may display your work in its portfolio, unless a written NDA or buyout is in place.

  • Archive

    Files are held for 90 days after delivery; retrieval after that is $95.

  • Crew & talent

    Crew sourced by E.A.T. Media carries a 15% coordination fee. Client-sourced crew is the client's liability.

  • Permits & location

    You're responsible for site access and power. E.A.T. Media coordinates FilmLA permits; permit, venue, and parking fees pass through at cost + 15%.

  • Insurance

    A certificate of insurance naming your venue as additional insured is available on request, at no charge.

  • Legacy pricing

    Existing clients on prior rates are honored for one booking cycle from this card's effective date, then move to current pricing.

Common Questions

FAQ

Do you have a photo studio?

We shoot with our own backdrop setup — seamless white and black. If your project needs a dedicated studio space instead, we arrange it and pass the rental through at cost (see Add-Ons above).

How does booking actually work?

Start with a free 20-minute discovery call. A $75 hold reserves a full 60-minute strategy session, credited to your booking. A 50% non-refundable retainer then confirms your date.

How long until I get my photos or video?

Photo proofs land in 3–5 business days, retouched selects in 7–10. Video takes 10–14 business days from when we have all assets in hand.

Can I use the photos/video for ads or paid media?

The default license covers personal and portfolio use. Commercial use — ads, paid media, packaging, out-of-home — needs the extended commercial license add-on.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel?

One free reschedule with 72+ hours' notice. Inside 72 hours, the retainer is forfeited. The retainer is non-refundable in all cases, and canceling within 7 days of your booked day means the full fee is due.

Do you travel outside Los Angeles?

Yes. Zone-based travel surcharges apply beyond 30 minutes from the studio — see Add-Ons & Licensing above for the exact rates.
