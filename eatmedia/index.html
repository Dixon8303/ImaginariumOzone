Get ready

Photoshoot & Media Readiness Prep

There are five areas where a little advance preparation makes a huge difference: Face, Hair, Hands, Skin, & Clothes.

Area 1 — Face

  • Whiten teeth: use strips or visit a dentist.
  • Don't pick: stop picking at skin or blemishes — wounds and scabs are trickier to cover with makeup than a simple pimple.
  • Exfoliate a couple of times before your appointment.
  • Get a facial at least a week before, to allow redness to heal.
  • Wax eyebrows at least a few days before the photoshoot.

Areas 2 & 3 — Hair & Hands

  • Hair: touch up roots 3 days prior, get a trim for neat, healthy hair, and deep condition.
  • Hands: get a manicure/pedicure — minimalists can use clear polish, and neutral colors work well. Clean your rings and jewelry (sonic cleaner or the toothbrush method).

Areas 4 & 5 — Skin & Clothes

  • Skin: nair, wax, or shave. Allow a few days to heal after waxing, or shave the night before.
  • Clothes: bring 5–6 outfits.

Outfit ideas

  • Something dark: black, navy, or charcoal.
  • Something light: angelic, soft, translucent, cream, nude, or light pink.
  • Something fun/funky/whimsical: emphasize your personal style.
  • Something sexy: a formal backless dress, pinup swimsuit, or lingerie.
  • Consider necklines: boat neck or off-the-shoulder to add variety.
  • Consider textures: sumptuous textures add a rich, tactile element.
  • Avoid prints: patterns are distracting and steal attention from you.
