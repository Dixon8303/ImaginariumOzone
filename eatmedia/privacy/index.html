Everything, All That Media LLC ("E.A.T. Media," "we," "us") is a Los Angeles-based media production studio. This policy describes how information is handled on EatMediaTv.com and across our published works and connected platforms.

We collect only what you choose to give us: your name, email address, and project details when you submit a contact or booking inquiry. Our website host may automatically log standard technical data (browser type, pages visited, approximate region) to keep the site running and secure.

Inquiry information is used solely to respond to you and deliver requested services. Technical data is used for site operation and basic analytics. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to anyone.

Our site links to social platforms (Instagram, YouTube, Pinterest, TikTok, Facebook, X) and to storefronts that process purchases of our published works (e.g., Payhip).
