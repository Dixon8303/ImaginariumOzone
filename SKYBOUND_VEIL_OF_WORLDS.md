## SKYBOUND: VEIL OF WORLDS  
## CREATOR BIBLE (WORK IN PROGRESS)  
Afrofuturist Sky RPG System Design Document  
   
⸻  
   
## 0. PROJECT IDENTITY  
A mobile-first, procedural sky exploration RPG built around:  
* Floating sky islands  
* Airship navigation  
* Crew-driven storytelling  
* Turn-based tactical combat  
* Living procedural discovery systems  
This is a **spiritual successor framework** (original IP), not derived from any existing copyrighted game.  
   
⸻  
   
## 1. CORE CREATIVE DIRECTION  
## 1.1 Afrofuturist Foundation  
The world is built from a principle of **future-memory reconstruction**:  
* Civilizations inspired by Black and Brown cultural aesthetics across global diasporas  
* Technology expressed through wind, crystal, bronze, brass, cloth, bone, solar sails, and bio-mechanical skycraft  
* Architecture that merges ritual geometry with orbital engineering  
* Language systems influenced by multilingual, creole, and ancestral tonal rhythm design  
The sky is not empty. It is **ancestral infrastructure still functioning beyond collapse**.  
   
⸻  
   
## 1.2 Character Ethos  
* Protagonists: Black and Brown explorers, engineers, navigators, historians, and sky captains  
* Societies: decentralized sky nations built from migration, survival, and discovery  
* Identity: not monolithic—cultural diversity across floating biomes is central  
**Antagonistic Force**  
* A pale-skinned imperial technocracy  
* Doctrine: order through containment, mapping, and control of all sky routes  
* Philosophy: exploration is instability, freedom is entropy  
* Aesthetic: sterile white alloy, angular geometry, gravity-locked fortresses  
This creates a visual and ideological contrast:  
* Warm adaptive sky cultures vs cold imperial compression systems  
   
⸻

## Pillar 1 — Floating Sky Exploration  
Procedural sky layers with:  
* Islands  
* Ruins  
* Creatures  
* Anomalies  
* Hidden civilizations

Ships are living ecosystems:

* Modular construction  
* Cultural visual identity per faction  
* Upgrades tied to discovery, not currency

Crew members are not stats—they are:  
* Memory holders

* Emotional systems (trust, rivalry, alignment)  
* Narrative triggers for emergent story arcs  
   
⸻  
   
## Pillar 4 — Tactical Sky Combat  
Turn-based system influenced by:  
* Wind direction  
* Altitude layers  
* Ship positioning  
* Crew synergy chains  
   
⸻  
   
## Pillar 5 — Discovery Economy  
Progression is driven by:  
* Mapping unknown space  
* Logging phenomena  
* Unlocking lost knowledge systems  
* Cultural restoration of sky history  
   
⸻  
   
## 3. WORLD STRUCTURE  
## Sky Layer Architecture  
* Low Sky: trade routes, inhabited floating cities  
* Mid Sky: mixed ruins, storms, exploration zones  
* High Sky: ancient relic infrastructure  
* Void Sky: unstable reality fields and anomalies  
   
⸻  
   
## Procedural Structure  
World generation is driven by:  
* Seeded sky grid  
* Noise-based island emergence  
* Biome assignment system  
* Event injection matrix  
* Route graph generation  
   
⸻  
   
## 4. SYSTEM DESIGN SUMMARY  
## 4.1 Chunk Streaming Model  
* Player-centered forward streaming  
* Active / preload / unload zones  
* Mobile-optimized LOD system  
   
⸻  
   
## 4.2 Event System  
Events are balanced across:  
* Combat encounters  
* Discovery moments  
* Treasure systems  
* Environmental storms  
* NPC narrative encounters  
* Fleet-scale battles  
* Rift anomalies  
Controlled via:  
* Frequency weights  
* Risk-reward scaling  
* Cooldown logic  
* Player behavior adaptation  
   
⸻  
   
## 4.3 UI Philosophy  
The interface is a **cockpit of perception**, not a menu system.  
Layers:  
* Top: world state (weather, altitude, risk)  
* Center: uninterrupted exploration view  
* Left: discovery feed (memory stream)  
* Right: minimap + route intelligence  
* Bottom: context-sensitive command bar  
   
⸻  
   
## 5. NARRATIVE STRUCTURE (FRAMEWORK)  
The story is not linear—it is layered discovery.  
## Core Narrative Motifs  
* Lost civilizations encoded in sky ruins  
* Imperial suppression of navigation knowledge  
* Re-emergence of ancestral sky routes  
* Memory restoration through exploration  
   
⸻  
   
## Antagonist Doctrine  
The pale imperial force believes:  
* The sky must be mapped completely  
* Unknown space is instability  
* Freedom creates fragmentation  
Their systems are built to:  
* Lock airspace  
* Control navigation nodes  
* Collapse independent exploration networks  
   
⸻  
   
## 6. PROCEDURAL SKY GENERATION SYSTEM  
## Core Systems  
* 3D sky grid segmentation  
* Density noise → island existence  
* Elevation noise → scale variation  
* Corruption noise → danger + anomaly probability  
* Biome scoring system  
   
⸻  
   
## Output Layers  
* Islands (static procedural meshes)  
* Routes (A* weighted sky graph)  
* Weather systems (state machine)  
* Event injection nodes  
   
⸻  
   
## 7. EVENT BALANCING MATRIX (HIGH LEVEL)  

| Layer    | Risk    | Reward  | Frequency |
| -------- | ------- | ------- | --------- |
| Low Sky  | Low     | Low–Med | High      |
| Mid Sky  | Medium  | Medium  | Balanced  |
| High Sky | High    | High    | Low       |
| Void Sky | Extreme | Extreme | Very Low  |
  
Events scale dynamically with:  
* Player progression  
* Crew strength  
* Exploration history  
   
⸻  
   
## 8. ECONOMY PRINCIPLE
