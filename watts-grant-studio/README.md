# Watts Grant Studio

Grant application packages, written to win, for the small education
organizations that can't afford a full-time grant writer.

## The Offer

Submission-ready grant application packages for small education nonprofits
and community-based organizations (CBOs) in South Los Angeles.

## The Buyer

Executive directors and program directors of education CBOs and
after-school programs with **$100K–$2M annual budgets** — organizations
doing real work with real outcomes, run by people who are also writing
their own grants at 11pm because they can't justify a full-time grant
writer's salary.

## The Deliverable

One complete application package per engagement, built against the
funder's own published RFP/NOFO and scoring rubric:

1. **Needs statement** — the case for why this problem, this community,
   this program, now — backed by data.
2. **Program narrative** — what the program does, who it serves, how it
   works, mapped directly to the funder's priorities.
3. **Logic model** — inputs, activities, outputs, outcomes, and impact in
   one page, internally consistent with every other section.
4. **Budget + budget narrative** — line-item budget with a narrative that
   justifies every cost against the program described.
5. **Evaluation plan** — how outcomes will be measured, who's responsible,
   and how data feeds back into the program.
6. **Organizational capacity statement** — why this organization, with
   this staff and this track record, can deliver.
7. **RFP compliance checklist** — every eligibility rule, attachment,
   format requirement, and deadline, checked off.

## The Price

- **$2,500** standard, per full application package.
- **$750** first trial job — one real application, full deliverable, at a
  reduced rate so a new client can see the work before committing to the
  standard price.

## The Quality Bar

**Nothing ships below a self-scored 90% against the funder's published
rubric, with zero unmet requirements.**

Every package goes through a mandatory self-check pass (see
`pipeline/04_selfcheck.md`) before delivery: scored row-by-row against the
funder's own criteria, every eligibility and formatting requirement
verified, gaps flagged and closed, and the projected score re-checked
until it clears 90% with nothing left unmet.
