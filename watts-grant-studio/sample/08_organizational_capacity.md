# Organizational Capacity Statement — Rise & Read Watts

*(Mapped matrix rows: N7, N9, N11, N12)*

## Track record

Rise & Read Watts has operated continuously since 2019, serving 120
students each year at its current two Title I sites in the Watts/
Willowbrook corridor. In the most recently completed program year, 118
of 120 enrolled students completed at least 80% of scheduled sessions,
71% gained at least one full grade level in benchmark reading score, and
the organization held six family literacy nights averaging 84 attendees
each — the same targets and structure proposed in this application.

## Staffing and credentials (N7)

- **Program Director** — 9 years in out-of-school-time program
  management, including 5 years managing multi-site after-school
  literacy programming in Los Angeles County; holds a B.A. in Child
  Development.
- **Literacy Coordinator** — former classroom teacher (5 years, grades
  K–2, LAUSD), holds a Multiple Subject Teaching Credential and
  certification in the structured phonics curriculum used by the
  program.
- **Part-time tutors (10)** — all complete a 3-day paid onboarding in the
  program's phonics curriculum before working with students, pass
  Department of Justice/FBI background clearance and TB clearance prior
  to any student contact, and receive ongoing coaching from the Literacy
  Coordinator through the quarterly data-review cycle described in
  `07_evaluation_plan.md`.

## Program management (N11)

Daily attendance, safety check-in/check-out, and incident-reporting
procedures at both sites mirror each school's existing after-school
safety protocol, coordinated directly with each site's front office. The
Program Director carries responsibility for fiscal compliance and grant
reporting; the Literacy Coordinator carries responsibility for
instructional fidelity.

## Partnerships (N9)

Rise & Read Watts operates under signed site-use agreements with both
partner schools (attachment A5; one of two currently signed, the second
committed and in final signature routing — see
`09_compliance_checklist.md`), meets quarterly with each site's literacy
coach, and partners with each site's parent-teacher organization on six
annual family literacy nights.

## Fiscal capacity

Rise & Read Watts' current annual operating budget is $350,000, of which
this program (both sites, full school-year plus summer bridge) represents
the organization's core activity. The organization's most recent IRS 990
and board-approved budget (both on file, attachments A1 and organizational
budget document) show three consecutive years of balanced or surplus
operations and no history of audit findings.

## Sustainability plan (N12)

Beyond this grant period, Rise & Read Watts sustains the program through
a diversified funding base already in place: a multi-year local family
foundation grant covering approximately 15% of annual operating costs,
an annual individual-donor campaign anchored by a spring benefit event,
and in-kind facility and utilities support from both partner school
sites under the signed site-use agreements. No more than one funding
source — including this grant, if awarded — is budgeted to cover more
than 85% of any single program year's direct costs, consistent with the
organization's board-adopted funding-diversification policy.
