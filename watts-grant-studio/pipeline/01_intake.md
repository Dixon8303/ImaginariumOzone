# 01 — Client Intake Checklist

Purpose: collect everything needed to write a compliant, competitive
application before drafting starts. Missing intake items are the #1 cause
of late deliveries and low rubric scores — do not begin `02_extract.md`
until every "Required" item below is either collected or explicitly
marked as not applicable with a workaround noted.

## A. The Funding Opportunity

- [ ] Full RFP/NOFO document (PDF), including all appendices
- [ ] Published scoring rubric or evaluation criteria (if separate from
      the RFP body)
- [ ] Any FAQ documents, technical assistance webinar slides, or
      addenda released by the funder
- [ ] Link to the funder's application portal (if online submission)
- [ ] Prior year's RFP, if this year's hasn't been released yet, to
      draft against the expected structure

## B. Organizational Documents

- [ ] Most recent IRS Form 990 (or 990-EZ/990-N confirmation)
- [ ] Current year organizational operating budget
- [ ] Most recent audited financials or financial statements, if the RFP
      requires them
- [ ] IRS 501(c)(3) determination letter
- [ ] List of current board members with affiliations
- [ ] Organizational chart / staffing list with roles

## C. Program Data

- [ ] Number of students/participants served (current year and, if
      available, prior 2 years)
- [ ] Site(s) served: school name(s), address(es), district
- [ ] Demographic data on population served (free/reduced lunch %,
      English learner %, foster/homeless youth counts, etc. — whatever the
      RFP's priority populations require)
- [ ] Outcome data from the current or most recent program cycle
      (attendance, assessment scores, survey results, whatever the
      organization already tracks)
- [ ] Staffing for the proposed program: FTE count, roles, credentials
- [ ] Partner organizations, MOUs, or letters of support already in hand

## D. Prior Applications

- [ ] Any previously submitted application for this same or a similar
      grant (funded or not) — reuse consistent facts, avoid contradicting
      a still-active narrative
- [ ] Reviewer feedback from a prior unsuccessful application, if
      available
- [ ] Current grant/contract list (what else is funded, avoid double-
      counting the same costs across grants)

## E. Letters of Support

- [ ] Inventory of who can provide a letter of support (partner schools,
      partner orgs, elected officials, community members)
- [ ] Draft or existing letters already on file
- [ ] Confirmation of who still needs to be asked, and by when

## F. Logistics

- [ ] Submission deadline (date AND time, with time zone)
- [ ] Submission method (portal upload, email, physical mail)
- [ ] Point of contact at the client org for questions during drafting
- [ ] Target internal review date (minimum 3 business days before the
      real deadline, to leave room for the self-check pass)

---

**Gate to proceed to `02_extract.md`:** sections A and F fully complete.
Sections B–E may be completed in parallel with early drafting, but every
item must be closed before `04_selfcheck.md` can pass.
